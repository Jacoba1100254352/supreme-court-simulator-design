# Benchmark Extraction Scaffolds

This directory contains benchmark extracts, schemas, and source registries. Each artifact carries its own evidentiary boundary; some are direct primary-source slices, while templates and work queues are not validation evidence.

The benchmark files are deliberately separate from `data/calibration/`. Calibration files are normalized observations consumed by the simulator's guardrail reports. Benchmark extracts preserve row-level source evidence, while scaffold files define what a future extraction must contain before weak pathway rows can be upgraded.

Current scaffold:

- `emergency-application-linkage-schema.csv`: required fields for linking emergency applications to reason-giving, public disagreement, merits follow-through, and downstream implementation effects.
- `emergency-application-linkage-template.csv`: generated blank coding sheet whose columns are derived from the schema.
- `emergency-application-order-extract-shadow-docket-v3-0.csv`: compact OT2023-OT2024 Shadow Docket Database v3.0 full-court emergency-application extract with source-record identifiers, grants, and public-disagreement flags.
- `emergency-application-order-extract-shadow-docket-v3-0-manifest.json`: manifest for the compact emergency extract, including the raw CSV hash and filter.
- `emergency-application-grant-linkage-workqueue-v1.csv`: generated coding sheet for the 31 granted full-court emergency-application rows in the compact extract; merits linkage, status-quo effect, and downstream-policy fields are intentionally uncoded.
- `emergency-application-denied-linkage-workqueue-v1.csv`: generated coding sheet for the 200 denied and 10 non-binary/NA full-court emergency-application rows in the compact extract; it is the all-application closure queue and not validation evidence.
- `emergency-application-linkage-coded-v1.csv`: official-docket coded benchmark for all 31 granted source-record rows from the linkage queue, representing 30 unique emergency matters across OT2023 and OT2024. This is a completed granted-emergency linkage benchmark, but it remains grant-only and official-docket based rather than an all-application or external implementation benchmark.
- `emergency-application-denied-linkage-coded-v1.csv`: official-docket coded benchmark for all 210 denied/non-binary source-record rows from the denied/NA closure queue. This closes docket-visible all-application linkage fields for the compact OT2023-OT2024 emergency extract while remaining below external lower-court, agency, or policy implementation evidence.
- `emergency-application-denied-linkage-coded-v1-manifest.json`: manifest for the denied/NA official-docket linkage benchmark, including row counts, source URL pattern, fetch status, snapshot date, and the not-external-implementation boundary.
- `certiorari-term-flow-extract-journal-ot2023.csv`: compact official Journal OT2023 term-flow extract with source-record identifiers for paid/IFP docketed cases, plenary-review counts, and related statistics.
- `certiorari-term-flow-extract-journal-ot2023-manifest.json`: manifest for the compact Journal extract, including the official PDF hash and parsed statistics.
- `certiorari-term-flow-extract-journal-ot2024.csv`: compact official Journal OT2024 term-flow extract, including 1,327 paid, 2,527 IFP, 3,856 total docketed cases, and 70 plenary-review grants.
- `certiorari-term-flow-extract-journal-ot2024-manifest.json`: manifest for the OT2024 Journal extract, including the official PDF hash and all 22 parsed opening-statistics rows.
- `certiorari-docketed-cohort-ot2023.csv`: independently enumerated closed OT2023 paid/IFP docketed-intake cohort covering all 4,222 official public docket numbers, including 4,033 certiorari petitions with docket-visible filing, response/CFR, CVSG, amicus-presence, relist, and mature cert-stage outcome fields.
- `certiorari-docketed-cohort-ot2023-manifest.json`: cohort manifest recording the exact paid and IFP docket ranges, zero failed fetches, petition-type and disposition counts, source hash, reuse/fetch counts, and the undocketed-submission, counsel, issue, split-quality, and vehicle-quality boundaries.
- `certiorari-docketed-cohort-ot2024.csv`: independently enumerated closed OT2024 Journal-count snapshot cohort covering 3,854 paid/IFP dockets and 3,683 certiorari petitions, with three pending outcomes retained explicitly.
- `certiorari-docketed-cohort-ot2024-manifest.json`: cohort manifest recording the exact count-defined ranges, zero failed fetches, source hash, resumable-fetch provenance, and four same-cutoff-date public dockets above the published count ranges as explicit snapshot exclusions.
- `certiorari-journal-disposition-extract-ot2023.csv`: row-level OT2023 Journal certiorari disposition seed shaped like the certiorari cohort schema, with docket numbers, disposition dates, paid/IFP class, parsed lower-court text where visible, and manual-review counts surfaced when parser classification leaves any rows unresolved.
- `certiorari-journal-disposition-extract-ot2023-manifest.json`: manifest for the Journal disposition extract, including the official PDF hash, row count, unique docket count, disposition counts, and paid/IFP counts.
- `certiorari-journal-docket-detail-ot2023.csv`: bounded official Supreme Court docket-page join for 3,947 of 3,951 OT2023 Journal certiorari disposition rows, adding petition filing, response/CFR, CVSG, cert-stage amicus, relist, and merits-detail fields where visible while recording remaining static-page fetch failures.
- `certiorari-journal-docket-detail-ot2023-manifest.json`: manifest for the near-complete Journal docket-detail slice, including the 3,951-row Journal source denominator, the 3,947-row official-docket detail count, the 4 remaining failed static-page fetches, source URL pattern, and the not-closed-cohort boundary.
- `certiorari-granted-docket-detail-ot2023.csv`: bounded official Supreme Court docket-page join for the OT2023 Journal granted and GVR/remand certiorari rows, adding petition filing, response/CFR, CVSG, argument, and merits-outcome detail without claiming a closed filed-petition cohort.
- `certiorari-granted-docket-detail-ot2023-manifest.json`: manifest for the granted/GVR docket-detail slice, including row count, Journal granted/GVR reconciliation, source URL pattern, failed-fetch count, and the not-closed-cohort boundary.
- `certiorari-cohort-schema.csv`: required fields for converting certiorari term-flow, CFR/response, CVSG, elite-counsel/amicus, and split-quality source slices into petition-cohort rows.
- `certiorari-cohort-template.csv`: generated blank coding sheet whose columns are derived from the certiorari schema.
- `implementation-compliance-schema.csv`: required fields for post-judgment lower-court treatment, implementation/resistance episodes, monitoring-capacity rows, and emergency downstream implementation observations.
- `implementation-compliance-template.csv`: generated blank coding sheet whose columns are derived from the implementation/compliance schema.
- `lower-court-precedent-treatment-aggregate-v1.csv`: 876-row normalized public replication extract for Masood, Kassow, and Songer (2019), retaining aggregate Shepard's cited-only, followed, adverse, and distinguished treatment counts for formally argued 1995--2004 Supreme Court precedents through 2016. Its 223 source-flagged constitutional-issue precedents provide bounded direct aggregate doctrinal-uptake evidence, not event-level or practical-compliance validation.
- `lower-court-precedent-treatment-aggregate-v1-manifest.json`: source-version, CC0 license, checksum, row/column reconciliation, aggregate-count, term-count, and output-hash manifest for the precedent-treatment extract.
- `lower-court-environmental-treatment-events-v1.csv`: 191 deduplicated published CourtListener-linked federal citing-opinion documents in fixed 730-day windows after five salient environmental Supreme Court decisions. Public full text and source-decision context are available for 115 rows; the other 76 retain explicit unavailability reasons and search snippets. Directional values are automated candidates pending expert legal review.
- `lower-court-environmental-circuit-exposure-v1.csv`: 65 nationwide-applicability/published-citation-presence cells for the same five decisions and thirteen appellate circuits. A zero is no observed published CourtListener-linked citation, not empirical exposure, ignored precedent, or noncompliance.
- `environmental-practical-implementation-events-v1.csv`: five decision-level agency implementation episodes preserving the complete purposive sample and published classifications in Gurganus (2025), joined to verified official agency or Federal Register actions.
- `environmental-directional-treatment-review-queue-v1.csv`: all six automated directional candidates plus a deterministic ten-row citation-only sample, with expert-review fields deliberately blank and pending.
- `gurganus-2025-table-1-classifications-v1.csv`: structured five-row transcription of the published case classifications, with Table 1/case-study locators and record hashes.
- `environmental-implementation-cohort-v1-manifest.json`: query, deduplication, source-document, classification, coverage, denominator, limitation, row-count, and output-hash provenance for both environmental cohort layers.
- `ecthr-execution-monitoring-pending-leading-cases-v1.csv`: generated HUDOC-EXEC live-query snapshot of English pending leading cases under Committee of Ministers execution supervision, shaped like the implementation/compliance schema for the monitoring-capacity source slice.
- `ecthr-execution-monitoring-pending-leading-cases-v1-manifest.json`: manifest for the HUDOC-EXEC monitoring extract, including the API query, row count, source links, supervision-code counts, and snapshot date.
- `source-registry.csv`: candidate source families already named in the calibration materials, with their current evidentiary limits.

Generated protocol and queue reports live at `reports/benchmark-extraction-protocol-v1.csv` and `.md`, plus `reports/benchmark-extraction-workqueue-v1.csv` and `.md` for emergency rows and `reports/certiorari-extraction-workqueue-v1.csv` and `.md` for certiorari rows. `reports/environmental-implementation-cohort-summary-v1.csv` and `.md` reconcile the bounded environmental snapshot; `reports/environmental-full-text-availability-audit-v1.csv` and `.md` expose its nonrandom missingness. `reports/implementation-compliance-closure-plan-v1.csv` and `.md` distinguish bounded acquisition snapshots from the remaining expert-coding, relevant-case opportunity, remedy-fidelity, broader noncompliance, and emergency-downstream work. None supplies denominator-matched general compliance validation. The other generated certiorari, emergency, lower-court aggregate, and monitoring reports retain their narrower documented boundaries.
