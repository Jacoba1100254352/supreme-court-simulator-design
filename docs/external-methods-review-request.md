# External Methods Review Request

Use this before adding more simulator features. The goal is to find the strongest objections to the calibration posture, mechanism interpretation, and replication materials while the paper is still easy to revise.

## Suggested Reviewer Profile

Ask one quantitative judicial-politics, empirical legal studies, or computational social-science reviewer who is willing to be critical. The reviewer does not need to agree with simulation as a method, but should be able to evaluate whether the paper's claims are properly scoped.

## Materials to Send

- `paper/main.pdf`
- `REPLICATION.md`
- `paper/source-audit.csv`
- `reports/constitutional-review-campaign-v2.md`
- `reports/calibration-baseline.md`
- `reports/mechanism-ablation-v2.md`
- `reports/parameter-sweep-v4.md`
- `dist/constitutional-review-replication.zip`, generated by `make replication-package`

## Review Questions

1. Does the paper's design-search framing avoid overclaiming empirical validation?
2. Are the five expectations specific enough to make the campaign results interpretable?
3. Are the calibration guardrails defensible as plausibility checks, or do any read like unsupported validation claims?
4. Does the mechanism-level contrast table clarify institutional drivers, or does it risk implying causal estimates from observed courts?
5. Are the direct outputs and derived indices separated clearly enough?
6. Are the source-audit anchors adequate for the main literature, data, model, calibration, and result claims?
7. Does the replication package contain enough material to reproduce the analytic results reported in the paper?
8. What is the strongest reason a Journal of Law and Courts reviewer might reject the paper?

## Draft Email

Subject: Request for critical methods review of constitutional-review simulation paper

I am preparing a Journal of Law and Courts submission on a simulation model for comparing constitutional-review institutional designs. I am not asking for copyediting. I would value a critical methods read focused on whether the paper overclaims, whether the calibration and validation posture is defensible, and whether the replication materials would let a skeptical reader reproduce the analytic results.

The project treats the simulator as a design-search tool rather than a predictive empirical model. The manuscript includes explicit expectations, source-backed calibration guardrails, campaign results, mechanism-level paired contrasts, a source-audit file, and a replication package. The main thing I want challenged is whether those pieces are enough for a law-and-courts or empirical legal studies audience.

If you are willing, the most useful output would be a short memo or margin notes answering the review questions below, especially the strongest rejection argument.

## Requested Output

A 1-3 page memo is enough. Ask for severity-ranked comments rather than line editing:

- Major blocking concern
- Important but fixable concern
- Clarification request
- Optional improvement
