## Review Questions

1. Does the paper's design-search framing avoid overclaiming empirical validation?
2. Are the five expectations specific enough to make the campaign results interpretable?
3. Are the calibration guardrails defensible as plausibility checks, or do any read like unsupported validation claims?
4. Does the benchmark-readiness work queue identify the right next validation targets before stronger empirical language is used?
5. Would the emergency-application linkage protocol collect the fields needed to evaluate merits follow-through, reason visibility, and downstream effects?
6. Do the generated extraction work queues pick source slices that would actually close the top emergency and certiorari benchmark gaps?
7. Does the OT2023-OT2024 emergency-application reconciliation strengthen the denominator evidence without overclaiming merits-follow-through or downstream-effect validation?
8. Does the granted-emergency linkage work queue collect the right next fields for merits follow-through, status-quo effect, and downstream implementation?
9. Does the denied/NA emergency linkage work queue preserve the right 210-row source target for all-application docket-linkage review?
10. Does the official-docket coded OT2023-OT2024 granted-emergency benchmark show the right coding standard while staying clearly below an all-application, denied-application, or external implementation benchmark claim?
11. Does the official-docket coded denied/NA emergency benchmark appropriately close docket-visible all-application linkage fields while preserving the external-implementation boundary?
12. Does the emergency field-readiness matrix make the all-application docket-linkage, conditionally complete linked-merits, and still-missing external implementation evidence boundary clear enough?
13. Do the independently enumerated OT2023 and OT2024 cohorts justify bounded two-term docketed-intake language for 8,076 paid/IFP dockets and 7,716 certiorari petitions, while keeping the three pending OT2024 outcomes out of the resolved grant/GVR denominator?
14. Are the exclusions for undocketed submissions, constitutional-review-only inference, the four OT2024 same-cutoff-date dockets above the Journal count-defined ranges, issue area, counsel, split quality, vehicle quality, and partial merits fields prominent enough?
15. Does the current-docket/Journal reconciliation adequately distinguish a docket absent from the Journal disposition extract from a genuine outcome disagreement?
16. Do the older Journal disposition, 3,947-row docket-detail, failed-page retrieval, and granted/GVR artifacts remain useful as independent reconciliation slices without confusing the closed cohort's denominator?
17. Does the certiorari field-readiness matrix draw the right line between complete docket-visible fields, partial metadata/merits fields, and uncoded issue/counsel/split fields?
18. Are the direct OT2023-OT2024 CFR and CVSG rates appropriately treated as short-window descriptive calibration evidence rather than stable population parameters or causal estimates?
19. Does the certiorari closure plan now prioritize the right remaining source slices after the docketed-intake denominator gate was met?
20. Does the 876-precedent aggregate treatment benchmark support the manuscript's bounded doctrinal-uptake description, including the 223-precedent constitutional-issue subset, without being mistaken for an event-level exposure denominator or practical-compliance rate?
21. Is the mapping from aggregate followed-versus-adverse Shepard's treatments to proxy context for the synthetic case-average `lowerCourtCompliance` score appropriately labeled as a scale mismatch?
22. Is the five-decision environmental lower-court cohort reproducible from its explicitly published-only CourtListener queries, stable provider-cluster tie-breaker, retained citing-case/docket identifiers, snippets, unavailability reasons, and public-document provenance?
23. Is it methodologically appropriate to publish the six directional values only as automated candidates when 115 of 191 events have public full text, 109 are citation-only, and 76 remain unclear? The queue now includes all six candidates plus a stratified ten-row citation-only sample, but expert fields remain deliberately pending.
24. Does the 65-cell decision-circuit frame now properly read as nationwide applicability and published-citation presence, not empirical exposure, opportunity, uptake, ignored precedent, or compliance?
25. Does the five-episode practical cohort and structured Table 1 transcription preserve Gurganus's purposive classifications while making clear that 4/5 compliant and 1/5 narrowly compliant describe case-study composition rather than probabilities?
26. Do the implementation/compliance schema, closure plan, and workqueue now give the environmental published-citation and practical snapshots only descriptive evidentiary credit while retaining expert-coding, remedy-fidelity, relevant-case opportunity, broader noncompliance, and emergency downstream work?
27. Does the HUDOC-EXEC pending-leading-case extract support bounded monitoring-capacity language without sliding into lower-court compliance, practical implementation, or government-noncompliance validation?
28. Does the mechanism-level contrast table clarify institutional drivers, or does it risk implying causal estimates from observed courts?
29. Are the direct outputs and derived indices separated clearly enough?
30. Are the source-audit anchors adequate for the main literature, data, model, calibration, and result claims?
31. Does the replication package contain enough material to reproduce the analytic results reported in the paper?
32. What is the strongest reason a Journal of Law and Courts reviewer might reject the paper?

## Draft Email

Subject: Request for critical methods review of constitutional-review simulation paper

I am preparing a Journal of Law and Courts submission on a simulation model for comparing constitutional-review institutional designs. I am not asking for copyediting. I would value a critical methods read focused on whether the paper overclaims, whether the calibration and validation posture is defensible, and whether the replication materials would let a skeptical reader reproduce the analytic results.

The project treats the simulator as a design-search tool rather than a predictive empirical model. The manuscript includes explicit expectations, source-backed calibration guardrails, campaign results, mechanism-level paired contrasts, a source-audit file, and a replication package. It also includes a five-decision published-citation environmental snapshot, an explicit full-text missingness audit, automated treatment candidates pending expert review, a nationwide-applicability/citation-presence frame, and purposive practical agency-response case studies. The main thing I want challenged is whether those pieces are appropriately bounded for a law-and-courts or empirical legal studies audience.

If you are willing, the most useful output would be a short memo or margin notes answering the review questions below, especially the strongest rejection argument.
