# Metric Semantics v1

This table records the denominator or scale for each audit metric and separates empirical source comparisons from synthetic outputs and reading aids.

| Family | Metric | Source metric | Use | Compatibility | Simulator denominator/scale | Source denominator/scale | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| certiorari | `paidCertPetitionShare` | `paidPetitionShare` | loose_calibration | near_pathway_match | paid plus IFP certiorari-path petitions in the generated filed universe | all docketed paid and IFP matters in official term flow | conditional paid/IFP split is closer than all-case paidPetitionRate, but the simulator still excludes many non-review docketed matters |
| certiorari | `ifpCertPetitionShare` | `ifpPetitionShare` | loose_calibration | near_pathway_match | paid plus IFP certiorari-path petitions in the generated filed universe | all docketed paid and IFP matters in official term flow | conditional paid/IFP split is closer than all-case ifpPetitionRate, but the simulator still excludes many non-review docketed matters |
| certiorari | `paidCfrRequestRate` | `cfrRate_paid` | loose_calibration | near_pathway_match | paid certiorari-path petitions | paid petitions or paid cert-stage subset described by source | stage is modeled explicitly, but the source rows are term-flow summaries rather than closed petition cohorts |
| certiorari | `ifpCfrRequestRate` | `cfrRate_ifp` | loose_calibration | near_pathway_match | IFP certiorari-path petitions | IFP petitions or IFP cert-stage subset described by source | stage is modeled explicitly, but the source rows are term-flow summaries rather than closed petition cohorts |
| certiorari | `cvsgRequestRate` | `cvsgFrequency` | proxy_context | count_rate_mismatch | all generated filed matters | petitions receiving CVSG per year in historical study period | the source is a count-per-year anchor, so the simulator rate is only a low-frequency transition diagnostic |
