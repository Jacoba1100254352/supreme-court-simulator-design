# Benchmark Extraction Protocol v1

This protocol defines the source extraction needed to close the highest-priority emergency and certiorari benchmark gaps. It is generated from `data/benchmarks/`, `data/benchmarks/source-registry.csv`, and the pathway denominator audit.

| Rank | Target | Metrics | Current evidence limit | Required source unit | Required fields | Completion rule | Validation upgrade | First extraction slice |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | emergency merits follow-through | `meritsAccelerationPerEmergencyStayDocket; emergencyGrantConditionalRate` | research_synthesis/conditional_near_match/n=1; research_synthesis/mechanism_proxy/n=1 | one row per emergency application or interim-relief request | sourceKey; sourceRecordId; sourceUrl; term; docketNumber; dispositionDate; dispositionType; linkedMeritsDocket; meritsFollowThroughCategory | each granted or otherwise resolved emergency application has a disposition and linked-merits category | can convert merits-follow-through rows from mechanism proxy to direct benchmark or loose calibration | noncapital emergency applications with grants in the existing OT2003-OT2021 source window |
| 2 | emergency reason and public-disagreement visibility | `reasonedEmergencyOrderRate` | research_synthesis/mechanism_proxy/n=1 | one row per emergency disposition | sourceKey; sourceRecordId; sourceUrl; term; docketNumber; responseRequested; reasoningPresent; publicDisagreement | each disposition records response request, reasoning, public disagreement, and full-court referral when visible | can separate written-reasoning benchmarks from dissent or disagreement proxies | applications with public writings or noted dissents in the existing noncapital emergency source window |
| 3 | emergency downstream implementation effect | `emergencyDownstreamEffect` | research_synthesis/scale_mismatch/n=1 | one row per granted emergency application plus linked implementation observation | sourceKey; sourceRecordId; sourceUrl; term; docketNumber; applicantType; reliefRequested; reliefGranted; statusQuoEffect; linkedMeritsDocket | each row classifies status-quo effect, linked merits result, and downstream policy status | can replace peak-count context with a direct event-linkage benchmark | presidential or government emergency applications in the currently cited peak-period source row |
| 4 | emergency application denominator | `emergencyStayDocketRate; emergencyOrderRate` | raw_or_primary_summary/denominator_mismatch/n=32; raw_or_primary_summary/near_pathway_match/n=22 | one row per application with term and source-record identifier | sourceKey; sourceRecordId; sourceUrl; term; docketNumber; applicationDate; applicationClass; applicantType | application counts and order counts are separated from all Journal-order denominators | can turn the emergency-application presence row from denominator mismatch into a pathway-specific rate | 2003-2024 shadow-docket rows already summarized in the normalized calibration file |
| 5 | certiorari petition denominator | `paidCertPetitionShare; ifpCertPetitionShare; certiorariAdmissionRate` | primary_source_named_in_table/denominator_mismatch/n=2; raw_or_primary_summary/near_pathway_match/n=2; raw_or_primary_summary/near_pathway_match/n=2 | one row per docketed certiorari petition or term-flow petition record | sourceKey; sourceRecordId; sourceUrl; term; petitionType; paidOrIfp; dispositionDate; certDisposition; granted | closed petition cohort separates paid and IFP filing, disposition, grant, and set-for-argument status | can move paid/IFP intake and certiorari admission from term-flow guardrails toward petition-cohort benchmarks | OT2023-OT2024 official docketed cohorts complete; next add another mature term or an issue-coded constitutional-review subset |

| 7 | certiorari CVSG signal | `cvsgRequestRate` | raw_or_primary_summary/near_pathway_match/n=2 | one row per petition eligible for or receiving a CVSG | sourceKey; sourceRecordId; sourceUrl; term; respondentType; cvsgRequested; dispositionDate; certDisposition; granted | CVSG request, SG recommendation, Court follow-through, and disposition are coded on a known petition denominator | can replace count-per-year CVSG context with a petition-stage CVSG rate and recommendation benchmark | OT2023-OT2024 direct CVSG-request incidence complete; next add SG recommendation and Court-follow-through coding |
| 8 | certiorari elite counsel and amicus access | `specialistCounselRate` | peer_reviewed_or_scholarly_summary/mechanism_proxy/n=1 | one row per petition with counsel, former-clerk, and cert-stage amicus indicators when source permits | sourceKey; sourceRecordId; sourceUrl; term; specialistCounselFlag | counsel and amicus signals are coded without reducing the denominator to only high-success attorney subsets | can separate access/salience proxy evidence from direct specialist-counsel prevalence benchmarks | Feldman-Kappner filtered elite-counsel rows and Lazarus OT2005 non-SG amicus rows as bounded proxy slices |
| 9 | certiorari split-quality coding | `genuineLowerCourtSplitRate` | research_synthesis/denominator_mismatch/n=1 | one row per petition or conflict observation with alleged and genuine split coding | sourceKey; sourceRecordId; sourceUrl; term; allegedSplitFlag; genuineSplitFlag | alleged split, genuine split, split depth, maturity, and disposition are coded in the same petition or conflict sample | can test alleged-versus-genuine split logic before treating split quality as a direct petition benchmark | Beim-Rader conflict rows as a secondary split-quality proxy before locating underlying petition records |
