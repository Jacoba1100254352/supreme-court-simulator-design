Manuscript use:

- This benchmark supports a bounded statement that the granted OT2023-OT2024 full-court emergency-application queue has official-docket coding for application timing, response requests, public disagreement, reason visibility, status-quo effect, merits linkage, and docket-derived downstream status.
- It can support grant-queue merits-follow-through and reason-visibility diagnostics for the 31 source-record denominator, including same-order certiorari grants, later certiorari or jurisdictional follow-through, merits judgments, a GVR, application-only stays or vacatur orders, and one pending certiorari-deadline extension.
- Combined with the separately coded 210-row denied/NA slice, it supports bounded all-application docket-visible linkage diagnostics for the compact 241-row source window. It still cannot support external lower-court, agency, or policy implementation conclusions beyond docket-derived downstream status.
